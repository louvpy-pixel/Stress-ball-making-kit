Questions? We’ve Got You 💖

Quick answers to the things RYOXZEL creators ask us most.

Great question! 💖

We spent a lot of time testing different glues for our TPR skins. Some didn’t bond well, some could damage the material, and others had strong odors or ingredients we didn’t feel comfortable including in a children’s craft kit.

So instead of adding a glue we weren’t fully confident in, we chose to leave it out for now.

The exciting news is that we’ve developed our own specially formulated glue designed for TPR skins. It was created to solve the bonding, material compatibility, and odor issues we found during testing, and it has passed children’s safety testing. ✨

Once it becomes available, customers who have already purchased a RYOXZEL kit can contact us with their Amazon Order ID, and we’ll be happy to provide one at no charge.

Option 1 — Zip Tie · RecommendedHave one person gently hold and stretch the neck of the skin while another person pulls the zip tie as tight as possible. Once secure, carefully trim off the extra tie.
Option 2 — Rubber BandUse the flaring tool to stretch the rubber band open, then carefully slide it over the neck of the skin. Adult assistance is recommended for this method.
Option 3 — TPR-Compatible GlueYou can also secure the cap using a glue specifically designed for TPR material.

Glue is not currently included in the kit. Our specially developed RYOXZEL TPR glue will be available separately once final product details are ready.

Until then, please use only a glue clearly labeled as compatible with TPR material.
